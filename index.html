How it works

  1. Send your request

    Item name, a photo or link, the shop or pop-up, and quantity. Use the template at the bottom.

  2. Get a quote and agree to the terms

    I check availability and purchase rules, then send a quote.

  3. Payment 1: item + buying fee

    Paid before the shopping run. Your order is confirmed once this arrives.

  4. I buy it on the next shopping run

    Runs happen on a fixed day each week. You get photos of the item and receipt.

  5. Payment 2: packing + shipping

    I pack and weigh your parcel, then send the exact shipping cost.

  6. Shipped with tracking

    You receive the tracking number and packing photos.

Terms

By sending payment 1 you agree to these terms.

If the item is sold out
  • Item price is fully refunded.
  • Half of the buying fee is kept, because the trip to the shop was made.
  • Random or blind items (trading badges, random stands) cannot be chosen. You get what comes out.
Cancellations
  • Until the day before the shopping run: full refund, minus payment processing fees.
  • After the item is bought: no cancellation.
Shipping, loss and damage
  • Every parcel is shipped with tracking. I photograph the items, packing and label before sending.
  • Uninsured methods (like Air Packet): I'm not responsible for loss or damage in transit.
  • Insured methods: I file the claim with the carrier for you. Compensation comes from the carrier.
Customs and import duties
  • All import duties and fees in your country are paid by you.
  • Please check that your country allows the item. If a parcel is refused or returned, return shipping is paid by you.
Returns and exchanges
  • No returns or exchanges after purchase.
  • If I buy the wrong item, I fix it at my own cost.
Storage period
  • Items are held for 30 days after payment 2 is requested.
  • If payment 2 is not received by then, the items are disposed of or sold, with no refund.

What I don't take

Lottery or raffle sales Over the shop's per-person limit Tickets Unofficial or bootleg goods Items that can't be exported Bulk orders for resale

I follow every shop's purchase rules. This service is for fans who want an item for themselves.
